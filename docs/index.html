Features

Everything your pipeline needs,
fully automated.

From source footage to published video — Mr Martin handles every production step so you can focus on your content, not the toolchain.

Automated Video Processing

Downloads and processes your source videos automatically. Audio extraction, format conversion, and preparation handled end-to-end without manual steps.

AI Transcription & Captions

Powered by AssemblyAI for speaker diarization and GPT-4o for caption generation. Accurate, context-aware subtitles generated automatically for every video.

Adobe Express Animation

Generates professional animated video segments using Adobe Express. Dynamic text, transitions, and visual effects applied programmatically to every clip.

Direct TikTok Publishing

Publishes directly to your profile via TikTok's official Content Posting API. Respects your account's privacy and interaction preferences on every post.

Privacy-First Architecture

All data lives locally on your device. OAuth tokens, video files, and transcripts never leave your machine — except through direct API calls you control.

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor your pipeline live, track published video performance, and manage pending posts — all from a purpose-built desktop interface designed for creators.

Built for creator privacy

Before every post, Mr Martin queries your TikTok creator settings to ensure your privacy preferences are always honoured. Videos can be published publicly, privately, or sent to your inbox as drafts. No content is ever shared with third parties, aggregated, or displayed on any public website. Your data is yours — always.

How it works

From raw footage to published video
in three steps.

1

Connect your TikTok

Authenticate securely via TikTok OAuth (Login Kit with PKCE). Your credentials stay on your device — Mr Martin never stores them on any external server.

2

Process your footage

The app extracts audio, transcribes speakers, generates captions, and produces animated clips via Adobe Express — fully automated, no manual steps required.

3

Publish to TikTok

Your finished, animated, captioned video is published directly to your TikTok profile — or sent to your inbox as a draft for final review before going live.
