2. Description of Service

Mr Martin is a desktop application designed for independent content creators and social media professionals. The App automates video production — including transcription, captioning, and visual animation — and enables users to publish their own content directly to their own TikTok profiles via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The App is a tool that individual creators use to manage and publish content to their own TikTok accounts. Each user authenticates independently with their own TikTok credentials. The App does not manage TikTok accounts on behalf of the developer or any third party.

3. TikTok Integration and Authorized Scopes

The App integrates with TikTok's official APIs. By connecting your TikTok account, you grant the App the following permissions, used exclusively on your behalf:

  • user.info.basic — to display your TikTok profile (open_id, display name, username, avatar) within the App interface, confirming which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to send finished videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts, for you to review and publish manually when direct posting is unavailable.
  • video.publish — to post completed videos directly to your own TikTok profile with an auto-generated caption.
  • video.list — to retrieve your own published video performance data (views, likes, comments, shares) and display it within the App's analytics dashboard, so you can monitor the performance of content you published through the App. This data is never displayed on any public website.

You may revoke any of these permissions at any time through TikTok's Settings > Security > Apps and Websites.
